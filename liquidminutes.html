Liquid Minutes

Only two of them are aware of the timelines...

A guy who calls himself Cork has been jumping timelines outside of his control for eons. He is almost always extremely loyal to his soulmate, who he calls Screw. Screw is not aware of the timeline jumps.

A guy who'll call himself Rack once lived an entire lifetime with a girl. Then, he started all over again, without her. And he restarted again. And again. And each time it's different. And each time they learn a little more about themselves. And eventually, she'll learn that the girl she loved hardly cared about her at all.

He was just a one-time fling for Cork, after all.

Cork

Cork is a guy who's been stuck in a sudden timeline hopping loop for who knows how long. He doesn't let it phase him though (usually). He loves his best friend Screw, even if he doesn't know if they're really the same one from his original timeline.

Cork is a bit of a daredevil and loves making everyone laugh. Despite their outgoing demeanor, they struggle to form actual meaningful relationships with new people. They also have a skewed sense of time and come off as surprisingly knowledgeable when people get to know them. Their whole multiverse orbits around Screw.

Screw

Screw's a person who has been unknowingly part of a sudden timeline hopping loop for who knows how long. They don't remember any of it, but they get a lot of deja vu around their best friend / enemy / this complete stranger / however they know each other in this timeline, Cork (usually they're best friends).

Screw is careful and caring. They tend to be shy, but they're pretty smart. They're also a bit of a people-pleaser. Usually, Cork has to help them come out of their shell. However, their personality can vary greatly from this base description depending on the timeline.

Rack

Rack is a recurring background character who becomes conscious of the timelines after a fling (aka entire lifetime) with Cork. His pre-built backstory usually includes him moving to the United States as a pre-teen.

Rack is often deadpan and sarcastic. They always seem a bit tired, regardless of whether they actually are (physically, at least). She's easily annoyed, but she's more likely to complain to someone else than to say it to their face. He often volunteers to help people, but then becomes annoyed by their needs.

Pin

Pin is an occasional background character who begins appearing consistently after her friend Rack becomes conscious of the timelines. She hasn't mentioned her deja vu, so neither Cork nor Rack realize anything's up with her.

Pin's demeanor can vary greatly, but tends to fit into one of two archetypes: a confident optimist or a bored cynic. She manages to get along with the usual people regardless of her demeanor, though. Either way, she's also both active and creative. She usually likes bodybuilding and sewing.

Ahna

Ahna is an occasional background character who begins appearing more often after her friend Rack becomes conscious of the timelines. She isn't aware of them at all, though. Her best friend is her twin sister Sonny, even despite the major differences between them. If she exists in a timeline, so does her twin.

Ahna is friendly and bubbly, always lighting up every room she enters. She isn't putting on a façade or anything, she is just genuinely a very happy person. She makes many friends, but always sticks close to her sister. She usually ends up with the same group of friends in general, even.

Sonny

Sonny is an occasional background character who begins appearing more often after her friend Rack becomes conscious of the timelines. She isn't aware of them at all, though. Her best friend is her twin sister Ahna, even despite the major differences between them. If she exists in a timeline, so does her twin.

Sonny is quite the interesting character… She does anything and everything in her power as an act of self-interest, no matter how it may affect others. But for her, “self-interest” expands to include her group of friends: Ahna, Pin, and Rack, usually. The priority is technically in that order, but she tends to see them as a collective in this regard.
